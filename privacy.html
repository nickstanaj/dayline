Dayline Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

Dayline is a calendar viewer for iPhone. This policy explains what Dayline accesses and what happens to it. The short version: Dayline has no server, and your data never leaves your device except to go directly between your device and the services you connect.

What Dayline accesses

  • Apple Calendar (EventKit): with your permission, Dayline reads events from the calendars on your device so it can draw them. It never creates, edits or deletes events.
  • Google Calendar: if you connect a Google account, Dayline asks Google for read-only access (calendar.readonly) and your email address, which labels the account in the app. Dayline reads your calendar list, calendar colors and events. It never writes to your Google account.
  • Location: if weather is turned on, Dayline asks for your approximate location while the app is in use. It sends that location to Apple WeatherKit to get local conditions.

Where the data goes

  • Calendar events and weather are stored only on your device: in the app's private storage, and in a small shared container that Dayline's widgets read.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain, for this device only.
  • Dayline's developer receives no data. There are no analytics, ads or tracking, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • Network requests go only to Google (calendar data for your account) and Apple (weather).

Your control

  • Disconnect Google: Settings → Connections → your account → Disconnect. This revokes Dayline's access with Google and deletes that account's cached events. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Calendar and location permissions: change them any time in iOS Settings → Dayline.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of Dayline's data from your device.

Google API data

Dayline's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to display your calendar to you on your device. It is not transferred, sold, or used for advertising, and no person reads it.

Children

Dayline is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
