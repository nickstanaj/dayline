Dayline – Calendar Widgets for iPhone

Dayline turns your schedule into a timeline you can read at a glance. Meetings are drawn to scale: a 90-minute block is three times a 30-minute one, overlaps sit side by side, and free time looks free.

  • Extra Large, Large, Medium and Small Home Screen widgets, built for iPhone Duo
  • Keeps every Google event's own color; recolor Apple calendars your way
  • Apple Calendar and multiple Google accounts, read-only
  • Day, Week and Month views, side by side when unfolded
  • Local weather with today's high and low

Dayline has no server and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
