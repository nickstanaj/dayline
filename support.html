Common questions

How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Dayline, pick a size, and tap Add Widget. To change a placed widget, touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget.

How do I connect Google Calendar?
Open Dayline → Settings → Connections → Connect Google Calendar. Access is read-only. Some Google Workspace administrators block third-party apps; if sign-in is refused, ask your administrator or connect the account through iOS Settings → Calendar instead.

Some calendars are missing.
Calendars you can only view (for example, coworkers' calendars) start hidden. Turn them on in Settings → Connections → your account, or per widget with Edit Widget → Calendars.

The widget isn't up to date.
iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Opening Dayline updates them immediately. If the widget says "Updated … ago", open the app to refresh.

Weather isn't showing.
Allow location for Dayline in iOS Settings → Dayline → Location (While Using), and make sure weather is on in Dayline → Settings → Weather.

How do I remove my Google account?
Settings → Connections → your account → Disconnect. This revokes Dayline's access and deletes its cached events from your device.
